[38] (*) 3.1. Capstone Project name: 
[39] English: Autonomous Hospital Assistant Robot System for Outpatient Department
[40] Vietnamese: Hệ thống Robot Tự hành Hỗ trợ Khoa Khám bệnh Ngoại trú
[41] Abbreviation: MediAssistRover
[42] Context: 
[43] Outpatient departments often experience high patient volumes and repetitive support activities. Patients, especially elderly people and first-time visitors, may have difficulty locating examination rooms, laboratories, pharmacies, or understanding hospital procedures. Meanwhile, nurses and support staff spend considerable time providing directions, escorting patients, and transporting medical documents or samples. In addition, emergency situations and crowded areas require continuous monitoring. Therefore, an autonomous robot system is needed to improve patient experience and operational efficiency in outpatient departments.
[44] Proposed Solutions 
[45] Autonomous Hospital Assistant Robot System for Outpatient Department is an autonomous robotic assistant designed specifically for outpatient departments. The system provides navigation assistance, patient escort services, voice-based interaction, internal delivery support, emergency monitoring, and real-time robot management. Patients can request assistance directly from the robot through voice interaction or mobile applications. Hospital staff can assign tasks, track robot status, and receive emergency alerts. Administrators supervise robot operations and analytics through a web dashboard, while the backend system ensures task coordination and real-time communication.
[46] Functional requirement
[47] Module for Patient
[48] Request robot assistance for navigation guidance within the outpatient department or information support.
[49] Communicate with the robot via voice interaction for real-time assistance.
[50] Ask for outpatient-related information such as examination rooms, laboratories, pharmacies, payment counters, and medical procedures.
[51] Control the robot using voice commands, including start navigation, stop navigation, and request emergency assistance.
[52] Receive step-by-step navigation guidance to the desired destination through the robot interface.
[53] Request escort services to be guided to examination rooms, laboratories, pharmacies, or service areas.
[54] Receive emergency support by triggering SOS requests through voice or interface commands.
[55] Module for Robot
[56] Navigate autonomously within outpatient environments using mapping and localization.
[57] Perform path planning, waypoint navigation, and obstacle avoidance during movement.
[58] Escort patients to designated locations based on system or user requests.
[59] Execute voice commands and provide interactive voice-based assistance.
[60] Provide outpatient-related information such as examination rooms, laboratories, pharmacies, and service locations.
[61] Perform delivery tasks for documents, laboratory samples, medication, and medical supplies.
[62] Detect crowd density, heatmaps, and congestion areas for smart monitoring.
[63] Detect emergencies such as SOS signals, patient falls, or restricted-area intrusion.
[64] Generate alerts for navigation failures, blocked paths, or abnormal conditions.
[65] Perform identity verification using QR codes or face recognition for task authorization.
[66] Module for Staff
[67] Assign tasks to the robot for patient escort, navigation assistance, and delivery services.
[68] Coordinate robot operations to escort patients to examination rooms, laboratories, pharmacies, or payment counters.
[69] Manage transportation requests for documents, medication, laboratory samples, and medical supplies.
[70] Monitor the robot status, task progress, and operational availability in real time.
[71] Track the robot location and movement within the outpatient map system.
[72] Receive emergency alerts from the robot, including SOS signals and abnormal situations.
[73] Handle navigation failures, blocked paths, or operational errors reported by the robot.
[74] Confirm completion and status of escort and delivery tasks.
[75] Module for Admin
[76] Manage robot, operational status, and system configurations.
[77] Monitor real-time robot activities.
[78] Track robot locations, missions, and performance metrics across the outpatient department.
[79] Analyze crowd density and heatmap data for outpatient space optimization.
[80] Manage hospital maps, navigation zones, checkpoints, and restricted areas.
[81] Configure user accounts, roles, and system access permissions.
[82] Control robot authorization and authentication mechanisms.
[83] Monitor analytics including task completion rates, travel distances, and service duration.
[84] Track emergency incidents, alerts, and robot response performance.
[85] Generate reports and dashboards for operational and performance analysis.
